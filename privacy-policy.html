rippl

Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 2025  ·  Chrome Extension
rippl is a local-only browser extension. All data it records stays on your device, always. Nothing is ever sent to any server, third party, or cloud service.

What rippl collects

rippl records only the minimum needed to build a picture of how you use AI tools:

  • Which AI tool websites you visit (domain names only, e.g. chatgpt.com)
  • Time spent on each AI tool during a session
  • Activity type and time estimates you provide via micro-prompts

What rippl never collects

  • Your conversations, prompts, or any inputs you type
  • Page content or screenshots
  • Personal information or account details
  • Data from non-AI websites
  • Anything transmitted to any server — no network requests are made

Where your data is stored

  • All data is stored locally in your browser using IndexedDB
  • No data ever leaves your device
  • No analytics, no tracking, no telemetry of any kind

Permissions explained

rippl requests the following Chrome permissions, and only for the stated purpose:

tabs To detect which AI tool is active in the current tab (domain only, never content)
storage For extension configuration and preferences
idle To pause time tracking when you step away from your computer
alarms For periodic heartbeat checks to keep session timing accurate

Your controls

You are in full control of your data at all times:

  • Pause or resume tracking at any time from the extension popup
  • Export all your data as a file
  • Delete all data permanently with one click
  • Uninstalling the extension removes everything from your browser

Data retention

  • Sessions older than 90 days are automatically pruned from local storage
  • You can delete all data at any time via the extension settings
